FOUSH Restaurant POS & Operations System

A high-availability, offline-first restaurant management & Point of Sale ecosystem engineered for high-pressure dining operations.

Executive Summary

Traditional cloud-only POS systems completely freeze during internet outages, causing lost orders, delayed kitchens, and cashier chaos. To eliminate this critical operational vulnerability for "FOUSH Ibn Sayed Restaurant" (a high-volume traditional dining establishment), I architected and developed FOUSH—a bespoke, resilient POS and kitchen ecosystem running on a Dual-Engine Architecture. It combines a local LAN Node.js server with SQLite WAL concurrency for 100% offline-first reliability, direct TCP socket ESC/POS thermal printing, and continuous Firebase Cloud replication for global remote management.

02 / Problem Statement

The Operational Fragility of Cloud POS

Why off-the-shelf software repeatedly fails in high-throughput restaurant environments.

Internet Dependency Vulnerability

Cloud-only POS systems halt completely during ISP outages or bandwidth drops. In a busy dinner rush, a 10-minute disconnection leads to lost ticket sequencing, billing discrepancies, and kitchen bottlenecks.

Sluggish Browser Print Dialogs

Standard web-based systems rely on browser print modals that require manual confirmations and take 3–5 seconds per bill. This creates cashier queues and delays kitchen ticket dispatch.

Role Fragmentation & Data Exposure

Generic software forces waiters, chefs, and cashiers onto a single cluttered screen. This exposes sensitive profit margins and gross sales figures to floor staff while complicating simple tasks.

03 / System Engineering

The Dual-Engine Architecture

A localized high-speed network for zero-latency floor operations, paired with resilient cloud replication.

Engine 01 (Local Core)

Local Node.js & SQLite WAL

Operates locally inside the venue across the local Wi-Fi router. SQLite in Write-Ahead Logging (WAL) mode enables concurrent table orders, KDS ticket routing, and drawer tallies with zero internet requirement.

100% Offline-First Uptime
Engine 02 (Cloud Sync)

Firebase Realtime Sync

An asynchronous background replication queue syncs completed transactions, shift closures, and inventory state to Firebase whenever internet is detected, enabling remote owner visibility.

Global Remote Management
Hardware Sockets

Raw ESC/POS Thermal Sockets

Communicates directly over TCP port 9100 with kitchen and cashier thermal printers. Bypasses the browser completely to fire raw ESC/POS byte streams the millisecond an order is confirmed.

Sub-Second Printing

05 / Role-Isolated UX

5 Dedicated PWA Portals

Purpose-built interfaces engineered specifically for each operational job in the restaurant.

Station 1: Central Cashier POS & Billing Terminal Offline-First Core

Central Billing & Table Management Terminal

The central transaction hub for cashiers. Built for high-speed touch interactions, table map management, split payments, delivery driver dispatch, and raw thermal receipt printing.

Multi-Channel Fulfillment: Seamlessly handles Dine-In tables, Takeaway counters, and Home Delivery orders with driver tracking.
Direct Thermal Trigger: Instantly prints receipts via raw TCP sockets without invoking the browser dialog.
Shift Reconciliations: Enforces blind drawer cash counts and prints end-of-shift X/Z audit summaries.
Station 2: Kitchen Display System (KDS Screen) Zero Latency Queue

Real-Time Kitchen Order Dispatch (KDS)

Mounted on touch monitors inside the kitchen, replacing paper tickets with an interactive digital preparation queue designed for high-heat, high-speed environments.

Live Ticket Queuing: Color-coded order cards showing elapsed prep time, table numbers, and item modification notes.
Audible Order Chime: Synthesizes alert chimes upon receiving new tickets over the local WebSocket/polling stream.
One-Tap Completion: Tapping "Ready" immediately notifies the floor waiter and updates the customer waiting display.
Station 3: Mobile Waiter Tablet & Handheld PWA Mobile-First PWA

Handheld Table Ordering for Floor Staff

A lightweight, standalone Progressive Web App installable on any Android or iOS smartphone/tablet. Enables waiters to take orders directly at the guest table without returning to the cashier.

Visual Category Navigation: Large, responsive touch buttons categorized by clay pots (طواجن), appetizers, and drinks.
Table Assignment: Links orders directly to active tables and tracks table availability status in real time.
App-Like Standalone Mode: Runs full-screen with custom icons, zero browser URL bars, and aggressive local asset caching.
Station 4: Remote Owner Executive Dashboard (admin.html) Cloud Analytics

Off-Premises Business Intelligence Portal

Accessible via web browser from anywhere globally. Provides ownership with a high-level commercial cockpit tracking gross revenue, net profit, food costs, and payroll.

Profitability & Food Cost Auditing: Calculates true gross margin by matching sold dishes against raw ingredient purchase costs.
Staff Payroll & Attendance: Logs employee shifts, advances, deductions, and calculate net monthly salary balances.
Historical Analytics: Interactive Chart.js graphs displaying daily sales volumes, peak rush hours, and item popularity rankings.
Station 5: Role-Based PIN Access & Privacy Screen Access Security

Role-Based Access Matrix & Data Governance

Restricts access to confidential financial data through secure numeric PINs, ensuring staff can operate necessary floor tools while sensitive profits remain protected.

Executive Master PIN (Boss): Full unrestricted access to net profit margins, financial balance sheets, and wage controls.
Operational Manager PIN (Ops): Allows inventory stock-in, shift audits, and menu adjustments without exposing overall profitability.
Session Auto-Lock: Automatic timer locks admin views after inactivity to prevent unauthorized access on shared devices.
06 / Cost Accounting

Recipe BOM & Inventory Control

Stopping kitchen shrinkage and protecting gross margins through automated ingredient tracking.

Bill of Materials (BOM)

Every menu item (e.g. Clay Pot Beef with Onion) is mapped to its exact recipe ingredients: raw meat (grams), onions, cooking fat, and specialized spice blends.

Real-Time Stock Depletion

The moment an order transitions to "Prepared" or "Paid", raw stock levels are automatically decremented in the SQLite database, reflecting true inventory on hand.

Spoilage & Waste Logging

Kitchen supervisors can log burned, expired, or dropped food items separately. This differentiates legitimate recipe consumption from operational kitchen waste.

07 / DevOps & Automation

1-Click Self-Healing Operations

Ensuring non-technical restaurant staff can launch, troubleshoot, and maintain the system effortlessly.

01

1-Click Launch Script (start_foush.bat)

Launches the background Node.js process, binds the local network port, and opens the POS interface in full-screen kiosk mode with a single double-click.

02

Self-Healing Network Fixer

Automated repair script (اصلاح_جهاز_المطعم_تلقائيا.bat) that resets lingering socket bindings, resolves local IP port conflicts, and unblocks firewall ports.

03

Clean Deployment Bundler

Automated script (create_restaurant_version.bat) that packages a pristine production build ready for deployment on new restaurant hardware in under 2 minutes.

09 / Professional Takeaway

Bulletproof Systems Engineered for Commercial Reality

The FOUSH project exemplifies the core philosophy of Business Information Systems (BIS): Technology must adapt to the physical and operational reality of the business. By engineering an Offline-First Dual-Engine architecture, interfacing directly with thermal printers via raw TCP sockets, and isolating staff workflows into dedicated PWAs, this solution delivers 100% operational uptime, eliminates ticket errors, and protects business profitability.
