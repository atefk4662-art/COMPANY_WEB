YOC (Your Only Chance) Youth Travel Platform

An end-to-end AI-powered domestic travel booking platform engineered for tourism operators to automate youth exploration across Egypt through mood-based planning and instant digital payments.

Executive Snapshot

Project At A Glance

15-Second Executive Overview
🏢 Client & Scope

Youth Travel & Tourism Platform — Full-stack smart booking ecosystem covering 8 Egyptian travel destinations.

⚠️ Core Problem

Fragmented trip info across social groups, rigid travel packages, and slow manual payment confirmations.

💡 Built Solution

An AI travel ecosystem with "Discover Your Vibe" AI Planner, user-generated programs, and Stripe Checkout.

📈 Business Impact

100% automated booking receipts, instant multi-currency checkout, and seamless multi-destination itineraries.

Core Capabilities

Key Platform Features

From AI mood-based itinerary creation to direct e-commerce checkout.

1. Landing & Search Hero

Inspiring hero layout encouraging exploration with quick destination search across ancient wonders, oases, and beaches.

2. Destination & Activity Hub

Categorized trip filters (Camps, Activities, Hotels) for top spots like Dahab, Siwa, Luxor, and Aswan.

3. AI Planner ("Discover Your Vibe")

Smart questionnaire letting travelers pick their vibe: History & Culture, Sea & Relaxation, Desert Safari, or Budget Wanderer.

4. Community Trip Ideas

Enables local guides and experienced travelers to publish custom programs with pricing and full itineraries.

E-Commerce Architecture

End-to-End Booking & Checkout

Secure Stripe gateway integration with instant automated PDF booking receipts.

1. Program Overview & Itinerary

Clear day-by-day tour plan, date picker, guest selection, and automatic pricing calculation.

2. Traveler Information

Secure traveler details collection with transparent fee breakdown and cancellation policies.

3. Stripe Payment Gateway

Encrypted checkout enforcing card verification, 3D Secure authentication, and currency conversion.

4. Instant Booking Confirmation

Generates a unique Booking Reference with trip breakdown, paid status badge, and receipt summary.
