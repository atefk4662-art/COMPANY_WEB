YOC (Your Only Chance) Youth Travel Platform

An end-to-end AI-powered domestic travel booking platform designed to transform youth exploration across Egypt through smart recommendations, custom itineraries, and instant payments.

Graduation Project Defense & Team

Executive Summary

YOC (Your Only Chance) is an innovative travel ecosystem built to solve key challenges facing youth and domestic travelers in Egypt. Young travelers often struggle with fragmented trip information, rigid tour packages, and complicated booking processes.

Our team designed and developed YOC as an all-in-one web platform featuring an AI Travel Planner ("Discover Your Vibe"), a curated destination directory (Dahab, Siwa, Luxor, Aswan, Cairo, Giza, Hurghada, Gouna), a community trip-sharing hub, and a complete Stripe-integrated Checkout System with real-time automated booking confirmation receipts.

User Experience

Core Platform Features

Explore how YOC delivers a seamless experience from destination discovery to custom trip creation.

1. Landing & Search Hero

Inspiring hero layout encouraging exploration with quick destination search across ancient wonders, oases, and coastal beaches.

2. Destination & Experience Hub

Categorized trip filters (Camps, Activities, Hotels) and curated cards for popular spots like Dahab, Siwa, Luxor, and Aswan.

3. AI Planner ("Discover Your Vibe")

Smart questionnaire letting users pick their travel vibe: History & Culture, Sea & Relaxation, Desert Safari, City Pulse, or Budget Wanderer.

4. Community Trip Ideas

Enables local guides and experienced travelers to publish custom programs (e.g., Cairo History, Deep Diving in Dahab) with price per guest.

Systems & E-Commerce Logic

End-to-End Booking & Checkout Engine

Designed a secure, transparent step-by-step transaction flow tailored for local and international payment methods.

Step 1: Program Overview & Tour Plan

Detailed itinerary view breakdown (Day 1 Training, Day 2 Deep Diving), date picker, guest selection, and total pricing transparent calculation.

Step 2: Traveler Information

Guest contact form collecting full name, email, and phone number with clear fee summary and free 48-hour cancellation policy.

Step 3: Payment Method Selection

Flexible options supporting Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, Meeza) and local mobile transfers (InstaPay).

Step 4: Stripe Payment Gateway

Integrated Stripe Sandbox checkout enforcing SSL encryption, card verification, and automatic currency conversion.

Step 5: Instant Booking Confirmation Ticket

Generates a unique Booking Reference (e.g., YOC-7F39F831) with complete trip breakdown, date, location, paid status badge, and receipt summary.
