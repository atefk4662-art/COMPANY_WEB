Triple Seven PlayStation Lounge & Café POS

A purpose-built management system managing real-time PlayStation station timers, integrated café orders, and zero-leakage shift finances.

Executive Summary

Managing a high-traffic PlayStation gaming lounge and café involves fast-paced challenges: tracking multi-console timers (Single vs. Multiplayer rates), adding drinks and snacks to active player tabs, and ensuring zero cash shrinkage between staff shift handovers. I architected and developed Triple Seven from scratch (Zero Templates)—a custom management system featuring a live station timer engine, integrated Café POS, automated Shift Cash Drawer audits, and secure Tailscale VPN tunneling for real-time remote business monitoring.

02 / The Operational Need

Why Generic POS Fails in Gaming Lounges

Gaming centers operate very differently from standard retail shops, requiring time-based billing and integrated hospitality.

Complex Time-Based Billing

PlayStation sessions constantly shift between Single and Multi-player modes, normal stations vs. VIP rooms. Tracking match durations on paper or clocks leads to billing disputes and lost minutes.

Café & Snack Order Leakage

Players order hot drinks, energy drinks, and snacks throughout multi-hour gaming sessions. Without direct attachment to active station tabs, café items get forgotten at checkout.

Shift Discrepancies & Remote Blindness

Operating 16+ hours a day across multiple staff handovers creates cash drawer discrepancies. Owners need complete financial visibility into live revenues from their phones without being glued to the front desk.

04 / System Modules

The 4 Core Management Modules

A clean suite of tools engineered for cashiers, baristas, and business owners.

Live Gaming Stations & Console Timers

A visual dashboard grid displaying all active PlayStation 5 and PS4 stations. Each station card shows the active player, game title, live countdown timer, and current hourly billing rate (Single vs. Multiplayer). One click starts, pauses, or ends the session with exact-to-the-minute cost calculations.

Station Timer Engine

  • Dynamic Rate Switching: Shift between Single (e.g. $8/hr) and Multiplayer ($12/hr) mid-session seamlessly.
  • Visual Color Status: Active (Cyan), Available (Gray), Paused (Amber), Cleaning (Red).
  • VIP Lounge Management: Higher-tier hourly rate setups for private rooms and large screens.

Integrated Café & Snack Ordering

A touch-friendly cafeteria interface where staff can add hot coffees, cold beverages, energy drinks, and snacks directly to any active PlayStation station. Orders are tracked in a pending/ready queue for the barista and appended automatically to the final invoice.

Café Hospitality Hub

  • Station Tab Attachment: Eliminates separate bills; drinks are consolidated into the player's gaming tab.
  • Real-Time Stock Alerts: Automatically flags beverages and snacks reaching low inventory thresholds.
  • Profit Margin Tracking: Calculates purchase cost vs. selling price per item.

Shift Handover & Cash Drawer Governance

Enforces absolute financial accountability during staff shift transitions. The system separates gaming revenue from café sales, computes expected cash in the drawer, and requires staff to log handover counts to prevent cash leakage.

Shift Guard & Drawer Auditing

  • Expected Cash Calculation: Opening Drawer Float + Gaming Revenue + Café Sales - Petty Cash Outflows.
  • Blind Shift Handover: Outgoing cashier inputs physical count without seeing expected total first, eliminating tampering.
  • Shift X/Z Reports: Instant printed summaries for staff records and owner archives.

Encrypted Tailnet VPN Remote Access

Using secure Tailscale VPN technology, the lounge owner can open the full management dashboard on their smartphone or laptop from anywhere. Provides live visibility into current occupied consoles, shift cash tallies, and monthly net profit.

Executive Remote Cockpit

  • Zero Public Internet Exposure: Database and server are hidden behind encrypted zero-trust Tailnet tunnels.
  • Live Occupancy Pulse: Check how many consoles are currently active and generating revenue in real-time.
  • Monthly Net Margin: Gross revenues minus rent, electricity, Wi-Fi, and staff wages.
05 / The Architecture

Custom-Built Software Architecture

Built from scratch without bloated templates for lightning-fast responsiveness.

Zero Templates 💻

Custom JavaScript State Engine

Engineered a bespoke lightweight state engine managing station countdowns, multi-timer states, and dynamic pricing calculations with zero lag.

Backend & DB ⚙️

PHP & MySQL Transaction Core

Structured relational database tables linking sessions, café inventory items, staff accounts, and financial shift records with strict ACID transactional integrity.

Zero-Trust Network 🛡️

Encrypted Tailscale Mesh Tunneling

To provide secure remote management ("Lounge in your pocket"), the system routes all off-premises connections through private Tailscale mesh tunnels, keeping the local venue server invisible to external internet threats.
