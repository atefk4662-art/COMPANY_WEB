01 / Operational Challenge

Why Generic POS Fails in Gaming Lounges

Gaming lounges require live time-based billing tightly coupled with hospitality and shift control.

Dynamic Time-Based Billing

Sessions shift between Single and Multiplayer rates mid-game. Tracking minutes manually leads to disputes and lost revenue.

Snack & Drink Leakage

Players order beverages throughout long sessions. Without direct attachment to station tabs, items get forgotten at checkout.

Shift Handover Blindness

Multiple staff shifts cause cash discrepancies. Owners need complete live financial visibility from their phones.

02 / Core Modules

The 4 Core Management Modules

Built from scratch without templates for maximum speed and simplicity.

Station Timers Engine

Live second-by-second countdown for PS5/PS4 consoles with 1-click Single vs. Multiplayer dynamic rate switching.

Key Timing Capabilities

  • Dynamic Rate Switching: Shift between Single and Multiplayer mid-session seamlessly.
  • Visual Status Colors: Live visual cues for Active, Available, Paused, and Cleaning.
  • VIP Lounge Setup: Tiered hourly rates for private rooms and large screens.

Café Hospitality Hub

Attaches hot & cold drink orders directly to active gaming tabs, with real-time stock deductions.

Hospitality Flow

  • Station Tab Attachment: Eliminates separate bills; drinks are consolidated into player tab.
  • Real-Time Stock Deductions: Automatically tracks remaining soda, energy drinks, and snacks.
  • Fast Touch Interface: Quick 2-tap ordering for cashiers and baristas.

Shift & Drawer Auditing

Mandatory blind cash counts and automated X/Z reports ensure complete financial accountability across handovers.

Financial Governance

  • Expected Cash Formula: Opening Float + Gaming + Café - Petty Cash.
  • Blind Handover: Cashier enters physical cash count without seeing the expected sum first.
  • Instant Shift Reports: Shift summaries exported for owner auditing.

Encrypted Tailnet VPN

Zero-trust encrypted remote connection allowing the owner to monitor live revenue and occupancy from anywhere.

Remote Intelligence

  • Zero Public Exposure: Database and server hidden behind encrypted zero-trust mesh tunnels.
  • Live Occupancy Pulse: Check active consoles and revenue on mobile in real-time.
  • 24/7 Security: Protected against unauthorized external network scans.
